# Week 3 design plan (23-28 Nov 2026)

## What week 2 looked like
Each post finally had a drawn object, but all four used the same composition: header, headline block at the top, object floating in the lower half, footer. Objects were small (500-560 px wide) and sat flat on the page. The carousel cover had low reach (Metricool: 2 IG views vs 9 for the Maps image), and the reel's skip rate was 50%.

## What changes this week
1. Composition changes per post instead of one template: the object becomes the hero (700-900 px), cropped by the frame so it feels close and physical, and the headline takes a clear column or band around it.
   - P1 WhatsApp (dark, night): a phone held at a slight angle, bleeding off the bottom-right edge, showing a 21:40 message and the instant away reply. Headline in the top-left column. Memorable idea: the clock time is the story.
   - P2 Highlights carousel: cover shows three oversized Story Highlight rings as the whole image (the format itself is the thumbnail), interiors are light with a phone screen showing that highlight's story frame. Value-stack framework, exact count of 3.
   - P3 Reel "can a patient book from your bio?": a full-height profile mock that gets fixed live, line by line. First frame already shows the problem (vague bio) plus the on-screen question.
   - P4 Process (light): a horizontal week strip Mon-Sat with four post tiles and a single "Approved" seal. Headline as a large two-line statement at the bottom third, so the grid alternates top-heavy (P1) and bottom-heavy (P4).
2. Depth and light: soft directional shadow, a thin specular edge on devices, gentle glow only behind the hero object. No glass blobs, no gradient text, no sparkles (Ola Derm feedback: avoid anything that looks "very AI" or busy).
3. Hierarchy: one headline per canvas (English 92-112 px Cormorant, Arabic 56-64 px Naskh 700 with manual line breaks), one supporting line per language at most, everything else lives inside the object as UI text.
4. Contact: footer now carries the WhatsApp Business number +216 93 871 805.
5. Reel: hook text and the problem visible at t=0 (skip rate 50% last week), a visible change every 2-4 s, captions above y=1440.

## Self-critique checklist
Orphan Arabic word, text touching or overlapping the object, crop that cuts a meaningful UI line, footer crowding, unequal margins, more than two accent colours, anything that reads as template filler.
